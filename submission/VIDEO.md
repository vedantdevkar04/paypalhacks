# Demo video: script and shot list (target 2:45, hard limit 3:00)

Rules that bite: under 3 minutes, public or unlisted on YouTube, no third-party music or trademarks.
Record your own screen and voice only. Show the web chat and WhatsApp's chat area, not the Instagram
logo or UI.

Before recording: open https://hiclose.onrender.com once to wake the server, click **New customer**
in the chat, and have the sandbox buyer login ready in an incognito window. Agent replies take up to
15 seconds, so **cut the waiting in editing**.

Tips:
- Record each row as its own clip, then join them. It's much easier than one long take.
- Zoom in on the important text (the price, "Paid ✅"), so it's readable on a phone.
- If a reply takes long, cut it. If the AI words something oddly, retake that clip.
